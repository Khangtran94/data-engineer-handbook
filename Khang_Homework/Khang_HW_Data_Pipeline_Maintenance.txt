# Week 5 Data Pipeline Maintenance  
## Runbooks and On-call Planning

---

## Pipeline 1: Profit

**Business Focus:**  
- Unit-level profit (for experiments)  
- Aggregate profit reported to investors

**Data Types:**  
- Revenue from accounts  
- Expenses on assets and services  
- Salaries by team aggregated

**Owners:**  
- Primary Owner: Finance Team  
- Secondary Owner: Data Engineering Team  

**On-call Schedule:**  
- Rotate weekly among 4 Data Engineers  
- Finance team reviews once a month  
- Consider holiday buffer: during major holidays (e.g., end of December, public holidays), an additional backup engineer is assigned to reduce risk of failure going unnoticed  

**Runbook for Investor Reporting Pipeline:**  
- Pipeline aggregates profit metrics monthly for CFO and investors  
- Data must reconcile with official financial filings (accountant verification if discrepancies)  
- Reports generated on first week of each month  

**Potential Issues:**  
- Profit numbers may not align with filings due to delayed expense reporting or missing data  
- Data reconciliation errors requiring accountant check  
- Pipeline failures due to missing salary or expense inputs

---

## Pipeline 2: Growth  

**Business Focus:**  
- Aggregate growth for investors  
- Daily growth metrics for experiments

**Data Types:**  
- Changes in account status (new subscriptions, cancellations, renewals)  
- Number of licensed users increased/decreased  

**Owners:**  
- Primary Owner: Accounts Team  
- Secondary Owner: Data Engineering Team  

**On-call Schedule:**  
- Debug during working hours only (no formal on-call)  
- Weekly rotation for Data Engineers managing data updates  
- During holidays, ensure Accounts Team availability or assign backup Data Engineer to handle urgent fixes  

**Runbook for Investor Reporting Pipeline:**  
- Weekly summary of account growth rates and forecast renewals  
- Data completeness check to ensure time series continuity  
- Final growth numbers delivered monthly for investor reporting  

**Potential Issues:**  
- Missing time-series data due to skipped steps by Account Executive team  
- Incomplete data on subscriptions causing inaccurate growth metrics  
- Delays in data processing affecting daily experimental results

---

## Pipeline 3: Engagement  

**Business Focus:**  
- Aggregate engagement reported to investors  
- Daily engagement metrics used internally for feature experimentation  

**Data Types:**  
- User activity clicks and session times  
- Aggregated usage metrics across accounts  

**Owners:**  
- Primary Owner: Software Frontend Team  
- Secondary Owner: Data Engineering Team  

**On-call Schedule:**  
- Weekly rotation among Data Engineers (one per week)  
- Software Frontend Team designated contact for issues  
- Include overlap meetings of 30 minutes to onboard next on-call engineer  
- For holiday periods, pre-assign backup on-call to cover shifts  

**Runbook for Investor Reporting Pipeline:**  
- Engagement metrics must be complete within 48 hours of event timestamps  
- De-duplication of click events done to ensure accuracy  
- Monthly engagement report generated for investor presentation  

**Potential Issues:**  
- Kafka queue delays causing late arrival of click data  
- Kafka outages resulting in missing events  
- Duplicate event ingestion leading to inflated metrics  

---

## Pipeline 4: Unit-Level Profit (for experiments)  

**Business Focus:**  
- Detailed profit data at unit/account level to power AB testing experiments  

**Data Types:**  
- Per-account revenue and cost details  
- License usage and associated expenses  

**Owners:**  
- Primary Owner: Data Engineering Team  
- Secondary Owner: Finance Team  

**On-call Schedule:**  
- Rotate daily between 4 Engineers due to high data freshness requirements  
- Backup engineer assigned during holidays to ensure support coverage  

**Runbook for Experiment Pipeline:**  
- Pipeline must deliver clean, reconciled unit profit metrics daily  
- Data inconsistencies flagged and logged for review by Finance  
- Integrate with experimentation platforms for feature testing  

**Potential Issues:**  
- Delayed input data causing incomplete unit profit metrics  
- Reconciliation mismatches causing erroneous experiment results  
- Pipeline crashing due to spike in ingest volume  

---

## Pipeline 5: Aggregate Data for Executives and Investors  

**Business Focus:**  
- Aggregate profit, growth, engagement metrics combined for executive reporting and investor updates  

**Data Types:**  
- Joined data from revenue, accounts, engagement sources  

**Owners:**  
- Primary Owner: Business Analytics Team  
- Secondary Owner: Data Engineering Team  

**On-call Schedule:**  
- Intensive monitoring in last week of each month by rotating engineers  
- Additional oversight during key reporting periods (month-end, quarter-end)  
- Holiday coverage planned with backup engineers  

**Runbook for Investor Reporting Pipeline:**  
- Final monthly aggregated report prepared for CFO, executives, and investors  
- Address Spark join failures, stale data, NA or division errors through retries/backfills  
- Report delivered by first week after month-end  

**Potential Issues:**  
- Spark out-of-memory errors during large joins  
- Stale data due to incomplete backfills in source pipelines  
- Missing or null data causing report calculation errors  

---
